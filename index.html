About Us

A stone manufacturer and temple builder, preserving India's architectural heritage

KMG Stones is dedicated to quality stone solutions and traditional architecture. Our stone factory processes and prepares stone products for temple construction, parking areas and other construction requirements.

We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern stone-processing techniques to deliver quality products and reliable project execution — undertaking temple and stone projects across India according to the specific requirements of every client.
